WHY IT HAS TO EXIST

The last mile of accounting
was never built.

Small companies do simple books and owe simple filings, yet the path from one to the other runs through subscriptions, portals and professional fees that cost more than the accounting itself. The four failures below add up to one gap.

F·01

Software stops at the PDF

Bookkeeping tools produce reports, exports and dashboards, then hand you back the obligation itself. They leave the statutory filing, the one thing the company legally owes, as an exercise for the reader.

F·02

The free lanes are closing

Governments are retiring self-filing services in favour of software-only submission. In the UK, both the registry's web filing for accounts and the tax authority's free company return service are on the way out. That pushes small companies into commercial software whether they want it or not.

F·03

Priced for practices, not companies

Vendors build filing software for accountancy firms processing hundreds of clients, and price it that way. A single company with twelve transactions a year gets the same seat licence as a practice, or nothing.

F·04

Simple companies pay complex fees

A dormant or micro company owes a confirmation statement and a set of near-empty accounts. Paying professional rates every year to restate that nothing happened is the clearest market failure in small-company life.

THE MECHANICS

Four layers,
one machine.

Built the VF way: a deterministic core doing the arithmetic, models doing the judgement work inside a harness, and a gate in front of anything that leaves the building.

LAYER 1

Ledger core

A double-entry engine that runs local-first on the owner's machine. Deterministic arithmetic, full audit trail, no cloud dependency: the books never leave the building.

LAYER 2

Mapping engine

The judgement layer: open models classify transactions and map the trial balance onto each jurisdiction's statutory format and digital taxonomy. Model-assisted, never model-trusted: every mapping traces back to source entries.

LAYER 3

Filing adapters

One adapter per authority (company registry and tax office, per country), speaking each system's native submission protocol. Jurisdictions are plug-ins: the UK first, Malta and Germany on our own books next.

LAYER 4

The gate

The VF harness discipline applied to accounts: no figure files unless it reconciles to the ledger, and no return leaves without passing evaluation. Failed checks stop the filing.
