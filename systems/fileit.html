WHY IT HAS TO EXIST

The last mile of accounting
was never built.

Small companies do simple books and owe simple filings, yet the path from one to the other runs through subscriptions, portals and professional fees that cost more than the accounting itself.

F·01

Software stops at the PDF

Bookkeeping tools produce reports, exports and dashboards, then hand you back the obligation itself. They leave the statutory filing, the one thing the company legally owes, as an exercise for the reader.

F·02

The free lanes are closing

Governments are retiring their self-filing services in favour of software-only submission. In the UK, both the registry's web filing for accounts and the tax authority's free company return service are on the way out. Small companies will have to buy commercial software whether they want it or not.

F·03

Priced for accountancy practices

Vendors build filing software for accountancy firms processing hundreds of clients, and price it that way. A single company with twelve transactions a year gets the same seat licence as a practice, or nothing.

F·04

Simple companies pay complex fees

A dormant or micro company owes a confirmation statement and a set of near-empty accounts. Paying professional rates every year to restate that nothing happened is the clearest market failure in small-company life.

THE MECHANICS

Four layers,
one machine.

Built the VF way: a deterministic core doing the arithmetic, models doing the judgement work inside a harness, and a gate in front of anything that leaves the building. The harness and the engine are the two pillars every VF system ships on.

LAYER 1

Ledger core

A double-entry engine that runs local-first on the owner's machine. Deterministic arithmetic, full audit trail, no cloud dependency: the books never leave the building.

LAYER 2

Mapping engine

The judgement layer: open models classify transactions and map the trial balance onto each jurisdiction's statutory format and digital taxonomy. The system never trusts a model's word: every mapping traces back to source entries.

LAYER 3

Filing adapters

One adapter per authority (company registry and tax office, per country), speaking each system's native submission protocol. Jurisdictions are plug-ins: the UK first, Malta and Germany on our own books next.

LAYER 4

The gate

The VF harness discipline applied to accounts: no figure files unless it reconciles to the ledger, and no return leaves without passing evaluation. A failed check stops the filing.

QUESTIONS

Asked.
Answered.

Which countries does FileIT file in?

R·01 covers Malta, the UK, Germany and Ireland, filing direct to HMRC MTD, ELSTER, Malta CFR and Ireland ROS. In code today the UK and Malta jurisdiction packs are live and Germany and Ireland are stubbed. R·02 adds France, the Netherlands, Spain and Italy; R·03 adds multi-state US filing. Each jurisdiction is a plug-in adapter, one per company registry and tax office.

Does FileIT file the return for me, or only prepare it?

It files. The ledger runs from transactions to trial balance to statutory accounts to submission in one continuous line, with no export, no re-keying and no handover to a portal. Each filing adapter speaks the authority's native submission protocol, and every submission returns a registry acknowledgment stored against the filing. The gate stops any return that fails reconciliation or evaluation before it leaves.

Where do my books live?

On your own machine. The double-entry ledger core runs local-first with deterministic arithmetic and a full audit trail, and the books never leave the building. Classification uses local AI, never a third-party LLM. Your data is encrypted client-side, so the server never sees plaintext, every filing is Ed25519-signed, and the books export in one click: you own the data outright.

What does FileIT cost, and when am I charged?

Two founding tiers, both lifetime-locked: Filed at €29 / month for the first 500 slots (full books plus returns filed direct to registry and tax office) and Software at €12 / month for the first 1000 (ledger, reconciliation and AI classification without filing). Reserving costs nothing: no card, no charge until R·01 ships and you activate. If R·01 has not shipped by 2028-03-15, we release the reserve list.

What ships today?

As of 2026-09-15 the build carries 225 passing tests across fifteen engineering waves. Working code covers the safe-expression rule evaluator, 14 UK and 2×3 DE, MT and IE 2026-27 rules, the complete UK filing lane (VAT100, SA100 and CT600 with iXBRL), an HMRC MTD adapter with OAuth 2.0, a Postgres-ready persistence layer, TrueLayer PSD2 ingest, a 4-signal reconciliation engine, a 26-category transaction classifier and a live Svelte workstation preview. The end-to-end test walks the full path from bank ingest to mock-HMRC submission. The source repository is internal-only until R·01 launch, then graduates to public with the Permanence Guarantee attached.

Can my accountant review a return before it is filed?

Yes, from R·02. Accountant collaboration mode lets your existing adviser review before you file, alongside Xero, FreeAgent and QuickBooks import. At R·01 you approve every filing yourself: bank feeds are read-only, and nothing is submitted until it reconciles to the ledger and passes the gate. If your accountant already handles submissions, the Software tier gives you the ledger, reconciliation and AI classification without the filing layer.
