VF LegacyEverything you leave behind, closed by you.

Families that have lost someone in the last five years have stories about the 14-month bureaucratic ordeal of closing digital accounts. Apple Legacy Contact is Apple-only. Google Inactive Account Manager is Google-only. Everplans is US-only. No EU sovereign player exists in this category. VF Legacy is the answer: a sealed vault of your last wishes, a multi-signal trigger designed against both false positives and false negatives, and an executor packet aligned with the European Law Institute Model Rules on Digital Succession.

WHY IT HAS TO EXIST

The last mile of estate planning
is where everyone gets stuck.

A properly executed will handles the legal instrument. It does not handle the operational reality: 20-40 accounts spread across 20-40 platforms, each with its own legacy process, its own forms, its own SLAs. Bereaved families spend months clicking through unfamiliar interfaces in unfamiliar terminology, or paying professional fees to have someone else do it. Four failures, one gap.

F·01

Per-vendor silos with no coordination

Apple Legacy Contact, Google Inactive Account Manager, Facebook Memorialisation: each big platform has its own tool. None of them talk to each other or know about the other 25 accounts your relative had.

F·02

US-only + expensive

The category-leading tools (Everplans, Trustworthy) are US-jurisdictional and US-priced. European families in Malta, Berlin, Dublin, Milan get exactly nothing built for their legal frameworks.

F·03

No EU sovereign incumbent

Not one. Boomers age into this problem every year and there is nowhere in Europe to send them for a coordinated, sovereign, legally-coherent product. The ELI Model Rules on Digital Succession landed in 2025; the field is ready and still empty.

F·04

DIY = spreadsheets + trust

The current alternative is a note in a shared drive that Mom sort of updated three years ago plus a verbal understanding your sister was supposed to know about. At the moment it matters, no one in the family knows who was supposed to close the Netflix account or what to do about the crypto wallet.

THE LIFE ARCHIVE

The whole story of a life,
closed by you, opened for family.

Link your social media during your lifetime. VF Legacy continuously ingests your own photos, videos, posts and activity into the same sealed vault, encrypted client-side; VF cannot decrypt. At trigger, the executor packet unlocks a family-viewable Memoir Viewer with your years arranged chronologically, your places visible on a timeline, your voice narrating the pieces you chose. A curated experience the family lives through, assembled quietly over years while you're still here. Ten source platforms at R·01: Facebook, Instagram, Google Photos, Google Account (YouTube, Maps timeline, Contacts), Apple Photos, LinkedIn, X, Spotify, Strava, Steam.

Ingest continuously.

First-boot pulls everything slowly into the vault until it catches up. From then on: monthly refresh per opted-in platform via your OAuth token, or a GDPR Article 20 data-portability request where the platform doesn't offer direct API access. Per-platform per-data-type opt-in: DMs, dating apps and financial detail are off by default and never move in without your explicit choice.

Curate a story.

You mark the important moments. You silence what shouldn't be surfaced. You record your own voice or text over specific photos: "this is your mother's 40th birthday…" You choose which recipients see which sections. Some memories go to everyone; some only to your daughter; some to your grandchildren decades from now.

Deliver a memoir.

After trigger, the executor packet includes access to a private Memoir Viewer: chronological timeline, albums by year and location and close-circle person, on-this-day playback, your curated stories in your own voice. Physical output too: PDF export at R·01, printed photo book via POD partner at R·02, USB drive with organised folders. Never public, never AI-generated.

THE LIFE ARCHIVE · WHAT IT IS NOT

Six lines we will not cross,
no matter who asks.

The Life Archive is a dossier of your life. That is what makes it valuable to your family, and what would make it catastrophic in the wrong hands or the wrong product design. We baked six categorical, permanent rejections into the spec, the code, and the Terms.

N·01

No AI-composed narratives

The Memoir Viewer presents what you yourself wrote, photographed, recorded. No model, ours or a third party's, generates anything in your voice. That is deep-fake territory, and we reject it permanently.

N·02

No chatbot deceased relative

We categorically refuse to let anyone "talk to" a deceased user via their archive. If someone wants that, they can take the exported data and do it privately. VF Legacy does not provide the tool.

N·03

No public memorial hosting

The Memoir Viewer is family-private. No public URLs. No social-media re-posting from within the product. The family's view stays inside the family.

N·04

No dating-app content

We exclude it permanently from every platform integration we build. Some categories are not appropriate for post-mortem exposure.

N·05

No NFT or crypto-memorial mint

Categorical no. Legacy is not a tokenisation product. Nothing in your archive gets minted, listed, or traded.

N·06

No data-broker, advertiser or analytics sharing

VF cannot see the Life Archive. VF cannot sell it. VF cannot even count it beyond one opt-in category-count summary you grant to your own AI agent, and that scope reveals nothing about content.

WHAT IT CHANGES

From verbal understandings
to sealed, dignified action.

VF Legacy shifts the operational work of estate discharge from the six-week grief-heavy period after death to the calm hour when you sit down + record it. The executor opens a signed packet, reads a clean per-jurisdiction PDF, sends the pre-drafted letters. Multi-signal trigger design means it never fires when it shouldn't. The trigger requires humans.

The executor gets a packet.

Per-jurisdiction PDF drafted by counsel in that country. 30 pre-drafted platform letters ready to send. Categorised inventory with your action directive per item. No guessing.

Your material outlives us.

12-month Permanence Guarantee (extended from house-wide 90-day baseline because estate is a life event). Sealed vault format documented in a public interop spec. Migration path to national tools provided. If VF disappears, your vault stays readable, forever.

THE MECHANICS

Four layers,
around one signed release.

R·01 ships as a Rust core with FFI bindings to Swift + Kotlin, a Fastify backend that never sees plaintext, and a Next.js web console for setup + executor unlock. Two new sub-primitives added to `vf-privacy-kit`: `legacy-trigger` for 2-of-N threshold release attestations, and a per-recipient message-encryption pattern via VF Mail sealed transport. Like every VF system, it leaves the lab with the engine fitted and the harness tuned.

LAYER 1

Vault

SQLite on device, encrypted at rest with XChaCha20-Poly1305 keyed by your Argon2id-derived PIN. Stores the categorised inventory, credential-recovery pointers, per-recipient encrypted messages, and legal-document PDFs.

LAYER 2

Trigger state machine

Four independent signals: dead-man's-switch (default 90 days inactivity), 2-of-N trusted-contact attestation threshold, death certificate uploaded by executor, and explicit owner-initiated release for planned transitions. Threshold logic requires convergence.

LAYER 3

30-day grace

Once the threshold is met and two VF operators in different EU jurisdictions have signed the release, the packet doesn't ship for 30 days. Owner can veto with PIN. Trusted contacts can withdraw attestations. Multi-channel notifications throughout. Only after grace does the packet reach the executor.

LAYER 4

Executor packet

Delivered via VF Mail sealed transport. Per-jurisdiction PDF plus JSON. Executor unlocks with their own PIN (if a VF Legacy user) or with a one-time recovery share the owner distributed physically (safe deposit box or sealed envelope with lawyer). Contains everything needed to discharge the digital estate.

QUESTIONS

Asked.
Answered.

How does VF Legacy know when to release my vault?

Four independent signals feed a trigger state machine: a dead-man's switch (default 90 days of inactivity), a 2-of-N trusted-contact attestation threshold, a death certificate uploaded by the executor, and an explicit owner-initiated release for planned transitions. No single signal fires a release; the threshold logic requires convergence, and two VF operators in different EU jurisdictions sign the release.

What stops the vault opening while I am still alive?

Once the threshold is met and the release is signed, the packet does not ship for 30 days. During that grace period you can veto with your PIN, trusted contacts can withdraw their attestations, and multi-channel notifications run throughout. Wrongful trigger is named as the signature threat in the published threat model, with a five-layer defence stack.

What does my executor receive?

A signed packet delivered over VF Mail sealed transport: a per-jurisdiction PDF drafted by counsel in that country, plus JSON, 30 pre-drafted platform letters ready to send, and a categorised inventory with your action directive per item. The executor unlocks it with their own PIN or with a one-time recovery share you distributed physically, in a safe deposit box or a sealed envelope with your lawyer.

Which countries does VF Legacy cover?

Malta launch, with cross-border executor packets for Germany, France, Ireland and Italy on day one, each drafted by that jurisdiction's counsel. The product is aligned with the European Law Institute Model Rules on Digital Succession, adopted in 2025, and R·01 covers the top-30 EU consumer platforms. The launch target is 2027 Q2-Q3, after the per-jurisdiction legal work.

Can VF Empire read my vault or my Life Archive?

No. The vault is SQLite on your device, encrypted at rest with XChaCha20-Poly1305 keyed by your Argon2id-derived PIN. The Life Archive is encrypted client-side and VF cannot decrypt it. Six permanent rejections are written into the spec and the Terms: no AI-composed narratives, no chatbot deceased relative, no public memorial hosting, no dating-app content, no NFT mint, no data-broker sharing.

What if VF Empire disappears?

VF Legacy carries a 12-month Permanence Guarantee, extended from the house-wide 90-day baseline because an estate is a life event. The sealed vault format is documented in a public interop spec, and a migration path to national tools is provided. If VF disappears, your vault stays readable, forever. Terms § 8 warranty pays out on material breach.
