WHY IT HAS TO EXIST

The fall is a fact of ageing.
The cloud camera is not.

Falls are one of the largest hidden emergencies in the world, and the wish to age at home is nearly universal. The technology offered to bridge those two facts mostly asks families to point internet-connected cameras at their parents. That trade should not be the default.

F·01

The scale of the fall

The World Health Organization estimates around 684,000 people die from falls every year, with some 37 million falls severe enough to need medical attention. For older adults, the hours between falling and being found often decide the outcome.

F·02

Everyone wants to stay home

Surveys find about three-quarters of older adults want to age in their own homes. Families want that too; they lack a trustworthy way to know that today was a normal day.

F·03

Cloud cameras have already failed

The 2021 Verkada breach exposed live feeds from around 150,000 cameras, including inside care facilities. A camera feed that leaves the house is a camera feed that can leak. With grandparents, that risk class should be off the table.

F·04

Decline is quiet before it is loud

Before the emergency there is the drift: meals skipped, nights disturbed, movement slowing. Point sensors and panic buttons miss it; a system watching routine patterns can surface it while it is still a conversation.

WHAT IT CHANGES

Alerting when it matters.
Silence when it doesn’t.

Private Care Watch is a monitoring and alerting system: it tells families what happened and what is drifting. It does not diagnose, and it does not promise prevention. That honesty is the product.

Found in minutes.

Fall and no-movement alerting designed to collapse the time between an incident and someone knowing, the single variable families can change.

The drift made visible.

Routine baselines per household: sleep, movement, kitchen activity. The family sees the pattern bend early enough to act gently.

Privacy as architecture.

All processing on the home hub. No video leaves the house; by design there is no cloud feed to breach, subpoena or resell.

THE MECHANICS

The hub is the product.
The cloud is not invited.

VF builds the system so the most sensitive data there is, footage of your parents in their own home, never becomes network traffic. Everything that watches, thinks locally.

LAYER 1

The sensing layer

Radar, motion and door sensors first; optional cameras only where the household chooses them. Sensor selection favours modalities that never produce identifiable imagery at all.

LAYER 2

The home hub

A local unit running all detection models on-premise. Fall inference, presence tracking and routine modelling happen here; the device processes raw sensor data and discards it.

LAYER 3

The pattern engine

Longitudinal baselines built per person, per home. Alerts are judged against this household’s normal, which keeps false alarms low and real drift visible.

LAYER 4

The family surface

Phone apps carrying alerts and daily summaries: status and events, never a live feed. Each family member sees only what the older adult consented to share.
