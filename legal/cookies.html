This site uses no advertising cookies, no analytics cookies and no tracking of any kind. We build private-by-architecture systems, and the company website behaves the same way.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Related technologies (localStorage, sessionStorage) store data in your browser in a similar way. EU law (the ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC as implemented in Malta, together with the GDPR) requires consent for any storage that is not strictly necessary for a service you have requested.
