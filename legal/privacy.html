Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 September 2026 · VF Empire Corp Ltd, Malta, EU

VF Empire builds systems that are local-first and private by architecture, and we hold this website to the same standard. This policy explains what personal data we process when you visit vfempire.com, why, and the rights you have under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”).

2. What we process, why, and on what legal basis

This Site is a static informational site. It contains no advertising trackers, no analytics scripts, no social-media pixels and no third-party marketing cookies. The processing that does occur is minimal:

2.1 Technical access data (server logs)

When you visit the Site, our infrastructure provider (Cloudflare, Inc., acting through its EU infrastructure) processes technical data that your browser transmits: IP address, date and time of the request, requested resource, browser type and version, operating system, and referrer URL. This is necessary to deliver the Site securely, to route traffic, and to protect against abuse (e.g. DDoS mitigation).

  • Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in operating a secure, functioning website.
  • Retention: short-lived operational logs held by our infrastructure provider; we do not maintain our own long-term visitor logs.

2.2 Contact by email

If you write to info@vfempire.com, we process the personal data you choose to send us (name, email address, message content) to handle your enquiry and any follow-up.

  • Legal basis: Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to entering into a contract, where your enquiry concerns an engagement) or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in responding to correspondence).
  • Retention: for the duration of the correspondence and thereafter as required by statutory retention obligations (e.g. where the correspondence becomes part of a commercial engagement) or as needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

2.3 Consent choice (cookie banner)

Your accept/decline choice in our cookie notice is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) so that we do not ask you again. This value never leaves your device and identifies nothing about you.

  • Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; storage of the choice itself is strictly necessary to honour your preference.

Details on cookies and similar technologies are in our Cookie Policy.

Our providers may process limited technical data outside the European Economic Area (for example, on globally distributed edge infrastructure). Where that occurs, transfers are safeguarded by adequacy decisions of the European Commission (including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, where the provider is certified) and/or Standard Contractual Clauses pursuant to Article 46 GDPR.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (Article 15);
  • Rectification of inaccurate data (Article 16);
  • Erasure (“right to be forgotten”) (Article 17);
  • Restriction of processing (Article 18);
  • Data portability (Article 20);
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests (Article 21), including at any time to any direct marketing;
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent, without affecting prior processing (Article 7(3)).

To exercise any right, email info@vfempire.com. We respond within one month, extendable in complex cases as the GDPR permits.

6. Complaints

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, in particular in the member state of your habitual residence or place of the alleged infringement. Our lead authority is the Maltese Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDPC), idpc.org.mt.

7. No profiling, no automated decision-making

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR. This website makes no decisions about you.

8. Data security

The Site is served exclusively over TLS (HTTPS). Our internal handling of correspondence follows the same security discipline we engineer into our products: least privilege, controlled infrastructure, and encryption in transit.

9. Children

The Site is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

10. Changes to this policy

We will publish any revision of this policy, with a new effective date, at vfempire.com/legal/privacy. The version published there is authoritative.
