What resisted the prompt

  • Every render grew a mouth despite "no mouth" repeated three times in the prompt. FLUX-schnell fought me. Next pass I use stronger phrasing and a specific negative token.
  • Aesthetic drifted toward friendly-companion (Sony aibo, Anki Cozmo, Pepper) rather than advanced robotics with armature plates (Figure 02, Sanctuary Phoenix). The plate seams and mechanical joints are missing.
  • Eyes read anime, concentric ring pattern, rather than the subtle glowing dot I aimed for.
Two directions for pass 2. Tell me which and which frame is the starting point.

A. Lean into the friendly companion vibe. Pick the strongest frame (probably 02 or 04), refine, kill the mouth for real, keep the warmth.

B. Reset harder toward advanced robotics. Visible plate segmentation, articulated joints, subtle eyes not anime, same house palette but noticeably more machine, less mascot.
