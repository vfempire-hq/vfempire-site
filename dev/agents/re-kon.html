WHAT RE-KON DOES

Missions.
Not chat sessions.

Re-Kon is not a chat companion. It is a research operator. You give it a brief. It gives you a dossier. Everything in between is auditable, deterministic where possible, and stays on your machine. Typical missions are the ones you would otherwise pay a research subscription for.

M·01

Company or entity dossier

"Everything filed by or against Company X in the EU and UK in the last 18 months, with regulator citations and dated excerpts." Re-Kon returns a themed dossier with the evidence chain.

M·02

Competitor landscape

"Every direct competitor to Product Y in Market Z, with pricing signals, funding data, product positioning and coverage timeline." Re-Kon surfaces gaps and inconsistencies between sources.

M·03

Regulatory tracking

"Every regulatory change affecting Industry W in 2026, EU + member state level, with statute citations." Re-Kon dates everything and flags what is still in draft.

M·04

Press & source review

"Every credible-press mention of entity or person P since date D." Re-Kon separates primary reporting from re-syndication and flags confidence per source.

M·05

Technical due diligence

"Everything published about the stack behind Product Y, including public repos, engineering blog posts and incident write-ups." Re-Kon prefers primary sources and quotes verbatim.

M·06

Custom mission

Any research brief that fits the pattern of "plan, dispatch, ingest, distil". If you can describe the shape of the answer you want, Re-Kon can plan for it.

HOW IT WORKS

Six stages,
every mission, every time.

Every Re-Kon mission passes through the same six stages. Each stage stores its state locally, so a mission can be paused, resumed and inspected at any point. Nothing runs invisibly. Nothing runs remotely.

01
Brief

You describe what you want, in plain language. Optional structured fields.

02
Plan

Re-Kon breaks the brief into sub-questions with per-question search plans.

03
Dispatch

Searches run through your configured backend. Results dedupe and rank.

04
Ingest

Sources are fetched with robots.txt respect, extracted, hashed and stored.

05
Distil

Per sub-question findings are drawn from source excerpts, with confidence tags.

06
Dossier

Executive summary plus themed findings, exported as Markdown, PDF and JSON.

THE EVIDENCE CHAIN

Every claim,
tied to a source.

Every finding in a Re-Kon dossier points back to specific source excerpts, their retrieval timestamp and a hash of the full source at retrieval. A machine-readable JSON version of the dossier ships alongside the human-readable one, so downstream tools or compliance workflows can verify every claim without parsing prose.

Example: one claim, one evidence record

This is a shortened version of what the JSON export looks like. Every claim in the dossier is a record like this. Nothing in a Re-Kon dossier lacks a source reference; where evidence is thin, the confidence tag says so.

REQUIREMENTS

What Re-Kon
needs on your box.

Re-Kon is a native binary, roughly 40 MB, with a bundled SQLite store and an embedded local web UI. It does not need a service or a background daemon. It runs when you run it. Model and search backends are your choice, plugged in through configuration.

MACHINE

Modest hardware

  • Windows 10 or 11, macOS 13 or newer, mainstream Linux
  • x86_64 or arm64, 4 GB RAM free
  • 500 MB disk for the app and dossier store
  • Optional: more RAM and disk if you keep large dossier histories
MODEL BACKEND · BYO

Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint

  • Local: Ollama, LM Studio, vLLM, llama.cpp
  • Cloud with your key: Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Grok, Together, Fireworks
  • Per-mission choice of planner vs distiller model
  • No VF-provided model in the loop
SEARCH BACKEND · BYO

Pick one or many

  • SearXNG on your own hardware, recommended default
  • Brave Search API with your key
  • Serper (Google proxy) with your key
  • Kagi with your key

ASKED, ANSWERED

Six questions
every buyer asks.

Does Re-Kon send my queries anywhere?

No. Your brief stays on your machine. Search calls go to whichever backend you configure, using your key or your self-hosted SearXNG. Model calls go to whichever endpoint you configure, local or cloud, using your key. VF does not sit in that path and does not see the brief or the dossier.

Can I run Re-Kon fully offline?

You need internet access to reach a search backend, but you can point Re-Kon at your own SearXNG and Ollama and never send data to a third-party service. The licence itself does not need to phone home after activation.

How is Re-Kon different from Perplexity or Deep Research?

Those products are subscription cloud services that ingest your queries. Re-Kon is a native binary you buy once, run on your hardware, and configure to talk to whichever model and search backend you trust. Your queries never leave your machine except as the searches themselves, and the model call is under your control.

What does the evidence chain give me in practice?

Every claim in the dossier lists the exact source URLs, the retrieval timestamps, the quoted excerpts and a SHA-256 hash of the full source content at retrieval. This makes claims independently verifiable and makes tampering visible, which matters for regulated buyers and compliance workflows.

How does Re-Kon work with Auto-Mate?

If both live on the same machine, they discover each other through the local fleet bus at ~/.vf/agents/. Auto-Mate can classify an input as a research task and route it to Re-Kon in one keystroke, opening the completed dossier in a preview panel when Re-Kon publishes the mission-completed event. The full flow stays on your box.

What happens if VF Empire ever ends Re-Kon?

The Permanence Guarantee applies. Within 90 days of any end-of-life announcement, the full source is released under a permissive open licence, the last signed build remains verifiable in the public transparency log, and the dossier format is documented so any conformant reader can extract your archive. Your activated binary keeps working regardless.
