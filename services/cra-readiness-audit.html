WHY IT EXISTS

Sep 11 2026
is closer than it looks.

Most product teams in Europe first heard "CRA" at a trade show in 2025, read the Regulation once, and moved on. The enforcement date is now less than twelve months out. When national market-surveillance authorities knock, they will ask for four things — and most product teams cannot answer any of them without weeks of scrambling.

F·01

"Show us your technical file"

CRA Article 27 requires a technical file made available to national market-surveillance authorities on request. Most product teams do not have one that a regulator would accept.

F·02

"Show us your SBOM"

Annex I requires a Software Bill of Materials per release. Most teams have a `package.json` or a `requirements.txt` — not a signed SPDX + CycloneDX SBOM in the shape a regulator or downstream customer expects.

F·03

"Show us your vulnerability handling process"

Annex II requires a coordinated disclosure policy, 24-hour reporting of actively exploited vulnerabilities, and a defined patch SLA per severity. Most teams have "email us at security@" and hope.

F·04

"Show us your security-support lifecycle statement"

Article 10 requires purchase-time disclosure of the security-support end date + user-facing documentation of the update mechanism. Most product teams have not committed to a public support lifecycle.

COMMON QUESTIONS

What most first calls ask.

We're a non-EU manufacturer selling into the EU — does CRA apply to us?

Yes. CRA applies to products placed on the EU market regardless of manufacturer origin. Non-EU manufacturers must either establish an EU authorised representative or work through an EU importer. The technical file + Declaration of Conformity requirements apply identically. We can help you structure the authorised-representative path.

We already have ISO 27001 / SOC 2 / cybersecurity insurance — do we need CRA on top?

Yes. Those frameworks address information-security management for your organisation. CRA specifically addresses the product placed on the market — vulnerability handling for the product, SBOM for the product, security-support lifecycle for the product. They complement rather than replace CRA obligations.

Can we skip CRA if our product is B2B only?

No. CRA applies to products placed on the EU market regardless of the buyer type. B2B products face the same essential requirements + vulnerability handling obligations. Class II classification is slightly more common for B2B industrial products — meaning conformity assessment via Notified Body may be required rather than self-attestation.

What if the audit reveals a gap we can't close by Sep 11 2026?

The remediation roadmap prioritises exactly this question. You'll get an honest assessment of what has to ship before enforcement + what can be phased. In some cases the answer is "delay the EU launch of this SKU until compliant" — better to hear that now than in December 2026 from a market-surveillance letter.

Do you handle the actual conformity-assessment paperwork?

The audit hands you the technical-file structure + Declaration of Conformity template + gap analysis. Filing paperwork with a Notified Body (for Module H) or maintaining the technical file (for Module A self-attestation) is a separate engagement — we quote that after the audit if you'd like us to continue.

Can you sign an NDA before the kick-off call?

Yes. Standard mutual NDA signed within 24 hours of your request. If you have your own template we can review + turn around within 2 business days.
