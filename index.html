QUESTIONS

Asked.
Answered.

What does VF Empire build?
VF Empire designs complete AI systems — engines, harnesses and command dashboards that make open-source AI models perform reliably on hardware the owner controls. One discipline, applied across education, robotics, medical, privacy and more.
What is the Helmsman engine?
Helmsman is VF's orchestration engine: it routes work across models, gates every output against evidence, and self-heals failures. It is the core that runs the VF floor and ships inside every commissioned system.
What is S◉LOCK?
S◉LOCK is the first complete system to leave the VF line — a private AI operating system with creative and work packages, running local-first on hardware you own. One product of the house, and the proof of the method.
What is the VF AI Training Centre?
A working centre, not a lecture hall. Open-source models come in raw; complete systems walk out — engines fitted, harnesses tuned, dashboards live. It runs on the same floor where VF builds its own machines. First intake is forming now.
Do VF systems depend on the cloud?
No. VF systems are built local-first: models, data and controls run on machines the owner holds. Cloud capacity is an option when a client wants it — never a requirement, and never the place your data has to live.
Where is VF Empire based?
VF Empire Corp Ltd is incorporated in Malta, EU — based in Għajnsielem, Gozo — operating since 2019, with network companies in Germany and the United Kingdom.
How do I commission a system?
Write to info@vfempire.com with the problem. VF scopes the machine, builds it to tolerance, and hands it over with dashboards, documentation and training.
