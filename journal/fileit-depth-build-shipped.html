FileIT depth-build shipped, R·01 spec-complete

Build log13 September 2026VF Empire

In the early hours of 13 September the self-filing ledger page, now FileIT, linked its public repository at github.com/vfempire-hq/fileit and flipped its chip to spec-complete at R·01. The P·15 chip on the landing page followed a few minutes later.

The depth build is the documentation set the page lists as published on 2026-09-13. The engineering spec runs to thirteen sections: ingest normaliser, hybrid AI architecture, a rules DSL for the tax engine, filing adapters per authority, an MCP server so your own AI agent can query the ledger read-only, the human-AI collaboration model and the banned-dependency list. The threat model applies STRIDE across eight adversary tiers, from a nation-state attack on the signature supply chain to a hostile household adversary, with residual-risk analysis and a DPIA covering all four R·01 jurisdictions. A separate document sets the collaboration model: AI proposes, human disposes, with bright lines on what the AI will not do. The investor pack has eleven documents and states the ask, a €2-4m seed.

R·01 as specified is the full stack in four jurisdictions: bank, card, invoice and receipt ingest, a local AI classifier, a double-entry ledger, jurisdiction-specific tax engines and direct filing to HMRC MTD, ELSTER, Malta CFR and Ireland ROS, on web, iOS and Android. The four-layer architecture (ledger core, mapping engine, filing adapters, the gate) is the same shape the Guardian Shield spec follows, and the model never gets the last word: every mapping traces back to source entries and no return leaves without passing evaluation.

Two days later the first real code landed, recorded in the 73 to 98 tests entry. The principles behind the build are on the software page.
