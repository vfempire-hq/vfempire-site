Guardian Shield and FuelIT pages match R·01

Build log12 September 2026VF Empire

The last three commits of 12 September brought two product pages up to the state of their specs. The Guardian Shield page was refreshed to match the R·01 spec, the P·18 tile on the landing page gained the spec-complete chip and the five-subsystem description, and the FuelIT page was refreshed to its spec-complete state.

Guardian Shield R·01 ships five subsystems on infrastructure you own: a password vault (Argon2id-wrapped XChaCha20-Poly1305, plaintext never on the wire), a malware scanner (ClamAV and YARA, both local, files never leave the device), a WireGuard VPN with three VF-operated exits or your own Mullvad or IVPN config, a DNS and URL filter at the OS resolver, and a zero-knowledge breach watch where only the first five characters of a hash travel. Every update is signed twice in public, by two operators in different EU jurisdictions, then inspected for 15 minutes, then entered in a public Merkle-tree log. The documentation set, engineering spec, threat model across six adversary tiers, verification checklist and investor pack, is dated 2026-09-12.

FuelIT is the fuel-price navigator: official government price feeds from nine EU countries ranked against your route, your tank and your real range. It ships with NAV·IT, the house's own sovereign turn-by-turn engine, so the destination you tap never touches Google or Apple. The landing-page tile carries the same chip.

The verification claims on the Guardian Shield page are the ones the security page later made general across the house: files never leave the device, verifiable by packet trace; vault plaintext never on the wire, verifiable by canary-string trace.
