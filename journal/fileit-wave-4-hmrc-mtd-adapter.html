FileIT wave 4: the HMRC MTD adapter

Build log15 September 2026VF Empire

Wave 4 gave FileIT its first real filing lane. The HMRC MTD adapter shipped with OAuth 2.0 token refresh and submission clients for the two return types the mappers already cover, VAT100 and SA100. The adapter targets the HMRC sandbox by default; production is gated by environment so nothing can reach a live tax office by accident. The test count moved from 98 to 112.

The adapter is layer 3 of the four the page describes: a ledger core that runs local-first on the owner's machine, a mapping engine that turns the trial balance into each jurisdiction's statutory format, one filing adapter per authority speaking that system's native protocol, and a gate in front of anything that leaves the building. HMRC MTD is the first adapter because the UK is the first jurisdiction in R·01; ELSTER, CFR and ROS follow the same shape.

Two things the adapter does not yet have are written on the page next to what it does. Real OAuth client credentials still have to come from the tax authorities' developer hubs, and HMRC software recognition is a separate step before any live production submission. The page keeps the founding-tier pricing behind the same condition: no charge until R·01 ships and a filing has gone through each of HMRC MTD, ELSTER, CFR and ROS.

The submission path itself is the one the security page describes for every VF product: Ed25519 client signatures, origin-signed responses, and no third-party AI on user-data paths. The previous wave, where both mappers became real, is in the 73 to 98 entry; the next one took Malta live. The wider thesis is on the software page.
