HOW VF HIRES

Four steps.
Each one visible to you.

An AI does the first pass, a person makes the decision, and every step has a stated reply time. The contact page publishes the same commitments for every desk.

STEP 1

You write

One email to careers@vfempire.com. Attach what shows your work: a repository, a build log, a dashboard, a design file. Plain text is fine.

STEP 2

AI first-pass triage

Our own AI, running on hardware we own, reads the application and sorts it against what the floor needs. Nothing you send leaves our machines.

STEP 3

Weekly shortlist review

A person reviews the shortlist every week. Whatever the outcome, you get a reply within 3 business days of writing.

STEP 4

A conversation, then the floor

If there is a fit, we talk about the work and the terms. Commercial and employment terms are agreed in writing before anyone starts.

THE WORK

What the floor builds.
And what you would touch.

The model is the cargo; VF builds the machine around it. Everything below runs local-first on owned hardware, and every lane ends in a gate. The software thesis explains why.

Engines.

Helmsman, the steering engine for frontier-class open models: it installs, tunes and pilots large models on owned metal, routes work across them and self-heals failures. The core that runs the VF floor.

Harnesses.

Process supervision, self-heal loops, project memory and evidence discipline: the frame that turns a raw model into a worker. More than 60 supervised lanes run this way today.

Dashboards.

Fleets, lanes, costs and outcomes on one pane of glass. Control surfaces for the floor and for every commissioned system that leaves it.

The build slate.

Twenty-two systems in build or spec-complete, from VF Mail and NAV·IT to Guardian Shield, FileIT and VF Home. Each one leaves the lab with engine fitted, harness tuned, dashboard live.

Security work.

Public repositories scanned clean of secrets, 2-of-2 signed releases, reproducible builds, a paid bug bounty. The security posture is the standard every product ships against, and the fixed-fee services apply it to clients' products.

THE TRAINING CENTRE ROUTE

Learn on the floor.
Then work on it.

The VF AI Training Centre is a working centre on the same floor where VF builds its own machines. Open-source models come in raw and leave as complete systems: engines fitted, harnesses tuned, dashboards live.

Four lanes are planned: open-model fitting, harness engineering, dashboard and control design, and utilisation labs. The first intake is forming. Intake dates and fees go by email to everyone who registers interest, and applications for a place go to the same careers desk with the same 3-business-day reply.

The centre is one route onto the floor; direct application is the other. Both start with an email to careers@vfempire.com.

HOW WE WORK

Local-first.
Gated by evidence.

Three habits define the floor, the same ones every product page promises to customers.

Hardware we own.

Models, data and controls run on machines VF holds. Cloud capacity is an option when a client wants it, never the place data has to live. Your application is handled the same way.

Nothing ships on a model's word.

Every lane ends in a gate: screenshots, checks, evidence. Humans hold decision authority. This is how VF reads the EU AI Act as an engineering specification, in section 4 of the Terms.

Built to outlive us.

Every product carries the Permanence Guarantee: source released, last build verifiable, data format documented, if VF ever stops building. The floor builds to that standard from the first commit.

QUESTIONS

Asked.
Answered.

Is VF Empire hiring right now?

There are no open roles on record today. VF publishes a role on this page when it is ready to fill it and emails everyone who has already written to careers@vfempire.com. A speculative application is welcome at any time: it is read by the careers desk, shortlisted weekly, and answered within 3 business days.

How do I apply to VF Empire?

Send one email to careers@vfempire.com with what you build, what you have shipped and which part of the house you want to join: engine, harness, dashboards, a system on the build slate, or the Training Centre. Attach or link to work that shows it. Our AI does the first-pass triage and a person reviews the shortlist every week.

How long does VF take to reply to an application?

Within 3 business days. The careers desk acknowledges receipt at once, the AI triage runs the same day, and the weekly shortlist review decides the next step. Every desk on the contact page publishes its reply time; the careers desk holds to this one whether the answer is yes, later or no.

Does an AI decide whether I get hired?

No. Our own AI, running on hardware VF owns, does the first-pass triage: it reads and sorts applications against what the floor needs. A person reviews the shortlist every week and makes every decision from there. Nothing you send is passed to a third-party AI service, in line with the Privacy Policy.

Can I join through the AI Training Centre?

Yes. The Training Centre runs on the same floor where VF builds its own machines, and applications for a place go to careers@vfempire.com like any other. The first intake is forming; intake dates and fees go out by email to everyone who registers interest, and the same 3-business-day reply applies.

Where would I work?

The floor runs local-first on hardware VF owns. Where a specific role sits, and on what terms, is stated in the role announcement and agreed in writing before anyone starts.
