Guardian Shield vs BitwardenA local-first security suite and a password manager, compared

Guardian Shield is a five-subsystem security suite from VF Empire (password vault, malware scanner, WireGuard VPN, DNS filter and breach watch) that runs on hardware you own, with every claim backed by reproducible checks. It is spec complete and in build; VF takes no payment until it ships. Bitwarden is a password manager from Bitwarden Inc., a United States company, with published client and server source code. This page compares the Guardian Shield vault with Bitwarden on where the vault lives, who holds the keys, business model, open-source status and jurisdiction.

DEFINITIONS

What each one is.
Scope first.

The two products overlap on one subsystem, the password vault. Guardian Shield adds four more, and Bitwarden has run as a shipping product for years while Guardian Shield is still being written. Both facts belong at the top of the page.

P·18 · VF EMPIRE

Guardian Shield

Guardian Shield rebuilds the consumer security suite on one principle: your protection runs on your hardware, answers to you, and is never anyone else's data business. R·01 ships five subsystems built on open primitives (ClamAV, YARA, WireGuard-Go, HIBP, OpenPhish, age, Argon2), with no commercial threat-intel SDK and no third-party analytics on any code path that touches user data.

The vault is Argon2id-wrapped XChaCha20-Poly1305, encrypted on your device, synced as sealed opaque blobs. It is also the primitive that seals VF Wallet, VF Legacy, VF Home and VF Mail. Guardian Shield is spec complete and audit-reviewed; the runtime is a five-to-seven-month build, and VF publishes an honest build status rather than a launch date.

Source: the Guardian Shield product page, its published spec and threat model, and the security posture.
BITWARDEN INC. · UNITED STATES

Bitwarden

Bitwarden is a password manager from Bitwarden Inc., headquartered in the United States. As Bitwarden describes in its documentation, vault data is encrypted on the user's device with a key derived from the master password, and the encrypted vault is synced through Bitwarden's cloud or through a server the user hosts.

Bitwarden publishes the source code of its client applications and its server on GitHub, offers a free personal plan alongside paid personal, family and business plans, and documents cloud data regions in the United States and the European Union. The product has shipped since 2016.

Source: Bitwarden's own public documentation. VF makes no claim about Bitwarden's security beyond what Bitwarden states.

THE DECISION

Choose by scope,
and by when you need it.

Choose Bitwarden if

  • You need a password manager today, on every platform, with a free plan.
  • You want to run the server yourself, using the self-hosting option Bitwarden documents.
  • You want client and server source code you can read now.
  • You need business features, such as organisation vaults, that a shipping product already provides.
  • A password manager is the whole requirement; you do not need a scanner, VPN, DNS filter or breach watch from the same vendor.

Choose Guardian Shield if

  • You want vault, malware scanner, VPN, DNS filter and breach watch as one local-first shield over every device in the house or the five-person office.
  • You run a de-Googled phone (GrapheneOS, /e/OS, LineageOS) and want a suite with zero Google Play Services dependency.
  • You want EU jurisdiction, a contractual Terms § 16 warranty and the Permanence Guarantee with source escrowed in Malta and Ireland.
  • You want to verify the claims yourself: packet trace, canary-string trace, two-signer check, transparency log, reproducible build.
  • You are willing to reserve a founding-tier slot now, pay nothing until R·01 ships, and hold VF to its published build status.

Guardian Shield is one of 23 systems on the VF build slate. The two other comparisons follow the same rules: VF Mail vs Proton Mail and NAV·IT vs Google Maps. QUESTIONS

Asked.
Answered.

Can I use Guardian Shield today?

No. Guardian Shield is spec complete and in build. What exists today is the published architecture (13 ADRs), threat model, privacy model and DPIA, verify-side crypto and CI hardening; the five subsystems' runtime code is a five-to-seven-month build. You can reserve a founding-tier slot with your email, with no card and no charge, and VF writes when R·01 ships and every claim is independently verifiable.

Is Guardian Shield only a password manager?

No. The vault is one of five subsystems. R·01 also ships a local ClamAV and YARA malware scanner whose files never leave your device, a wireguard-go VPN with three VF-operated exits, a system-wide DNS and URL filter rebuilt daily from public blocklists, and a zero-knowledge breach watch that sends only the first five characters of a password hash through a relay that strips your IP. Bitwarden is a password manager; the comparison on this page covers the vault only.

Where is my Guardian Shield vault stored?

On your device, sealed with Argon2id and XChaCha20-Poly1305. Sync between your devices moves opaque encrypted blobs of roughly 5 to 50 KB per user through VF infrastructure; the server never sees plaintext, which you can verify with a canary-string trace. If you lose the master password there is no recovery beyond an existing device, the 24-word code or the 30-day grace delay, by design.

What will Guardian Shield cost, and what does Bitwarden charge?

VF publishes two tiers on the product page: a founding tier at €39 a year, lifetime-locked, for the first 500 reservations, and a free tier forever with 20 vault entries, 5 breach lookups an hour and the default DNS blocklist. Nothing is charged until R·01 ships and you activate. Bitwarden publishes its own plans and prices on its own site; this page does not restate them.

What happens to my passwords if VF stops building Guardian Shield?

The Permanence Guarantee (ADR-0013) is contractual: VF releases the full source under a permissive open-source licence within 90 days, escrowed in Malta and Ireland with insurance-funded execution. The vault format is already a public spec with an open-source reference decryption implementation, every version ships an export lane, and signed packs continue for six months after any announcement. Your passwords stay readable after VF stops building.

Does Guardian Shield run without Google Play Services?

Yes. R·01 targets Android 10+ including GrapheneOS, /e/OS and LineageOS, with zero Google Play Services dependency, alongside iOS 16+, Windows 10+, macOS 12+ and Linux. The DNS filter binds to each platform's native resolver hook (PrivateDnsSettings on Android, NEDNSSettings on iOS, systemd-resolved on Linux, DoH on Windows), so the filter works without a vendor cloud in the path.
